Why a website

Your customers search on their phone before they call

"Dentist near me." "Cake shop in Kothrud." "Homestay in Lonavala." If they can't find you, or your page looks old, they call the next business.

No website? You look less trusted

A business with its own website looks established. Customers feel safer calling you.

Listing sites show your competitors

Justdial, Practo, Sulekha and booking sites often charge per lead or per booking, and they show other businesses on the same page as you.

Your own website brings enquiries straight to you

Your timings, photos, services and a WhatsApp button, all in one place. You pay once, and every enquiry comes to you.

Questions

Common questions

I'm not good with computers. Will I have to do anything?

No. You send photos and details on WhatsApp, and I do everything, including putting the website live.

Is there a monthly fee?

No monthly design fee. You pay once for the website. Only the domain and hosting are paid yearly to the provider, and I'll help you pick a good, low-cost option.

Is the free sample really free?

Yes. I make a sample homepage with your details. If you don't like it, you don't pay anything.

I'm already on Justdial / Practo / Google Maps. Why do I need a website?

Keep those too. A website is your own page, with no competitors next to you and no charge per enquiry. You can add its link to your Google listing, Instagram and visiting card.

Do you make CV websites for students?

Yes. A simple personal website with your skills, projects, education and a CV download button. Send me a WhatsApp message for the price.

How long does it take?

Usually a few days after I get your photos and details. A bigger website with more pages takes a little longer.

Can I change things later?

Yes. Just send me a message on WhatsApp with what you want changed.

Can I order only banners?

Yes. Banners are a separate service, priced per design.
